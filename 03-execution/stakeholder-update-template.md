# Stakeholder Update Template

> "Stakeholders don't want to manage your pilot. They want to know it's on track or needs their attention."

## Purpose

Stakeholder updates keep sponsors and key stakeholders informed without requiring their active participation in every meeting. Good updates:
- Take 2 minutes to read
- Clearly signal status
- Surface issues early
- Request specific actions when needed

---

## Update Frequency Guide

| Stakeholder | Frequency | Format | Purpose |
|-------------|-----------|--------|---------|
| Executive Sponsor | Bi-weekly | Written + brief call | Maintain support, escalate blockers |
| Steering Committee | Monthly | Presentation | Strategic alignment, major decisions |
| Business Stakeholders | Weekly | Written | Keep informed, gather feedback |
| Technical Stakeholders | As needed | Written | Technical coordination |
| Impacted Teams | Bi-weekly | Written | Change management, expectations |

## Steering Committee Presentation

### Format: 15-20 Minute Presentation

### Slide Deck Structure

```markdown
## Slide 1: Title
- [Pilot Name]
- Status Update - [Date]
- Presented by [Name]

## Slide 2: Executive Summary (1 slide)
- Overall Status: 🟢🟡🔴
- Primary Metric: [Current] vs [Target]
- Key Message: [Single most important point]
- Ask of Committee: [If any]

## Slide 3: Metrics Dashboard (1 slide)
- Primary metric trend chart
- Secondary metrics summary
- Guardrails status
- Kill criteria status

## Slide 4: Progress Highlights (1 slide)
- 3-4 key accomplishments
- Visual if possible (screenshot, diagram)

## Slide 5: Challenges & Mitigations (1 slide)
- Top 2-3 risks or issues
- Current mitigation actions
- Any escalations needed

## Slide 6: Timeline & Milestones (1 slide)
- Visual timeline
- Completed milestones ✅
- Upcoming milestones
- Any schedule risks

## Slide 7: Decisions/Input Needed (1 slide)
- Specific questions for committee
- Options with pros/cons
- Recommendation

## Slide 8: Next Steps (1 slide)
- Key activities next period
- Expected progress
- When to expect next update
```

### Presentation Tips

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Start with the headline | Bury the lead |
| Show, don't tell (use charts) | Wall of text |
| Be honest about risks | Hide bad news |
| Have a clear ask | Ramble without purpose |
| Stay within time | Let discussion derail |
| Know your backup slides | Get caught without detail |

## Communication Best Practices

### Tailoring by Audience

| Audience | Focus On | Avoid |
|----------|----------|-------|
| Executives | Business impact, decisions needed | Technical details |
| Business Owners | Process impact, results, change | Jargon |
| Technical Team | Architecture, integration, performance | Business strategy |
| End Users | What's changing, when, how to prepare | Why AI is cool |

### Status Transparency

**Be honest about status:**

| Real Situation | Status | How to Communicate |
|----------------|--------|-------------------|
| Ahead of plan | 🟢 | Celebrate but stay humble |
| On track | 🟢 | Confirm plan is working |
| Minor issues, recoverable | 🟡 | Note issue and mitigation |
| Significant risk, needs attention | 🟡 | Escalate with clear ask |
| Major problem, needs intervention | 🔴 | Escalate immediately |
| Kill criteria triggered | 🔴 | Pause and convene review |

### Escalation Language

When you need to escalate:

```markdown
**ESCALATION REQUIRED**

**Issue:** [Clear statement of the problem]

**Impact:** [What happens if not resolved]
- [Impact 1]
- [Impact 2]

**Root Cause:** [Why this is happening]

**Options:**
1. [Option A] - [Pros/Cons]
2. [Option B] - [Pros/Cons]
3. [Option C] - [Pros/Cons]

**Recommendation:** [What you think we should do]

**Request:** [Specific action needed from recipient]

**Deadline:** [When decision/action is needed by]
```

## Stakeholder Update Checklist

### Before Sending Any Update

- [ ] Status accurately reflects reality
- [ ] Key metrics current and verified
- [ ] Issues/risks disclosed
- [ ] Appropriate level of detail for audience
- [ ] Clear ask if action needed
- [ ] Proofread for errors
- [ ] Sent to correct distribution

### For Executive Updates

- [ ] Summary fits in 2 sentences
- [ ] Metrics visualized simply
- [ ] Blockers clearly escalated
- [ ] Decisions framed with options
- [ ] Sponsor had advance notice of bad news

### For Broad Distribution

- [ ] No sensitive/confidential details
- [ ] Accessible language (no jargon)
- [ ] Contact info included
- [ ] Reply path clear
